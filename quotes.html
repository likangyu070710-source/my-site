Nonsense

Fifteen statements that mean a great deal and say nothing at all.

Quotation of the visit

Tomorrow is another day, broadly indistinguishable from this one.

Anon.

  1. What goes up must come down, except in the cases where it does not.

    Anon., notebook, n.d.

  2. Less is more, except where more is required.

    From an unlabelled slide, c. 2019

  3. The best time to begin was earlier. The second best time is also earlier.

    Traditional

  4. A journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step, and continues with others.

    Marginalia, second-hand textbook

  5. Every ending is a beginning in disguise, though the disguise is rarely convincing.

    Anon.

  6. It is what it is, until such time as it is not.

    Overheard in a meeting room, 2024

  7. Everything happens for a reason, and the reason is usually Tuesday.

    Desk calendar, author unknown

  8. Silence is an answer, though never the one being waited for.

    Unattributed

  9. You cannot step into the same river twice, but you may step into a different one badly.

    Marginalia

  10. Time heals nothing; it merely continues, as it has done.

    Anon.

  11. The early bird gets the worm, and the worm gets an early morning.

    Traditional, revised

  12. If it is not broken, do not repair it; if it is, do not begin.

    Workshop wall, undated

  13. Knowledge is knowing what to say. Wisdom is knowing when not to. Understanding is knowing that nobody asked.

    Anon.

  14. Wherever you go, there you are, and there is where you were heading.

    Anon.

  15. Tomorrow is another day, broadly indistinguishable from this one.

    Anon.

No quotation on this page is attributed to a real person. None of them were improved by being written down.
