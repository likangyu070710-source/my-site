Ideas

Six things that would work, and would not help.

  1. 01

    The Sentence That Ends Itself

    A page that writes one sentence and then deletes it, word by word, while you read. By the time you reach the end there is nothing left to disagree with.

    Status · proposed

  2. 02

    A Clock With Fifty-Nine Seconds

    Every minute loses one second, so the clock is always slightly wrong and never wrong enough to complain about.

    Status · proposed

  3. 03

    The Library of Unread Books

    A catalogue of books that have never been finished. Each entry shows the first page, and the page on which the last reader stopped.

    Status · half-built

  4. 04

    An Alarm That Apologises First

    It rings, says sorry, explains that it would rather not, and then rings again. You wake up feeling guilty instead of rested.

    Status · abandoned

  5. 05

    A Map of Doors You Did Not Open

    It draws the route you walked and marks every door you passed without entering. The map is mostly doors.

    Status · proposed

  6. 06

    A Guestbook Nobody Reads

    Visitors sign it. The signatures are stored, counted, and never shown to anyone — including the people who signed.

    Status · finished, and useless

None of these has been built. That is the point of a list.
